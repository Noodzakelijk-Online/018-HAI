| BH-11 | High (authentication) | IDP password recovery | Reset completion changed the password and cleared its token in separate writes, allowing replay after a partial failure or concurrent request. | BH-15 | High (source revocation) | connected sources and Google OAuth | A revoked source was excluded from retrieval, but its mutation guard checked only ownership. Focused service/HTTP/OAuth tests and isolated PostgreSQL lifecycle tests cover the boundary. |

## How to add an entry

Record: severity, area, a concrete reproduction or observation, and a status
(Open / Resolved / Won't-fix with reason). Never mark Resolved without evidence.
